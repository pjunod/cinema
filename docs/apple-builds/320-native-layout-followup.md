# Native Live TV and shelf layouts

Build: 160
Issue: #320

Give iPad Live TV a larger player and programme context beside channels,
stack the panes in portrait, and open Guide and Recordings above retained
playback. Phone title actions use a labeled adaptive grid; the Live TV view
selector and status no longer compete for one compressed line. Apple TV
shelves reserve room for mixed metadata and focus borders, allow two title
lines, and show a title fallback when artwork is unavailable.

The [delivery ledger](../clients/NATIVE-LAYOUT-FOLLOWUP.md) records the single
adversarial review and validation limits. This is a source build, not an upload.
