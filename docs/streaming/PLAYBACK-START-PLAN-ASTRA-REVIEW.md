A claim counter can rise while the title stays unplayable. Change C1(a) to use the same foreground/source-permission decision through claim, hashing, submission and artifact production. Cover the independent background-capacity check and preserve the CPU reservation (`crates/plurxd/src/transcode/manager/construct.rs:1406,1434,1452`). Keep the per-domain limits and foreground revocation across nodes. A preference on the order of *rows* cannot pick which of two polling *workers* acquires the same row. Define worker eligibility, preference and takeover against one shared claim. Prove B can claim A's targeted row, finish it under encoder load and deliver a verified artifact usable by A. Inject a foreground waiter during the read and verify it gets the resource without exceeding the domain limit. **3. Keep a hard total bound, distinguish obsolete backlog from runnable capacity, and show current requests can enqueue and finish. A temporarily unreachable worker is insufficient evidence to retire its digest. Reuse the existing internal activity digest and freshness/unknown handling before adding membership schema. Test mixed digests, a missing report, two reconcilers, and recovery at the cap on both stores.
