## 2. Decisions

1. **The plan is an argument, not a `SessionRequest` field.** §4.2 put `start_plan: Option<Arc<StartPlan>>` on `SessionRequest` with `#[serde(skip)]`. `SessionRequest` has 75 struct literals, several in `vod/` (A1/B1's surface), so a field would edit `vod/`; a last argument `plan: Option<&StartPlan>` makes "a property of the call" type-level, and the two assignment sites become the two plan origins, pinned (`every_plan_argument_is_pinned_by_call`, `HELPER_CALLERS`).
2. **§12-1 taken as recommended:** `remote_placement_ready` (ingress and owner) and the software-budget admission read answer from the plan. A changed `cluster.media_pool_enabled` or `transcode.software_pool_threads` reaches a start in flight at most one plan late; the binding covers both since D3t.
3. **One re-plan, where it costs nothing durable** (review B1). A stale ingress view is caught by the catalog's quorum read inside `resolve_plan`, before the claim: there the create closes its view and re-plans from Authority in the same request, and its answer is the second attempt's. The repeated steps are the selection upsert (an idempotent repeat of the same digest) and the attempt's G1 note. After the claim the plan has fixed the candidate, the recipe digest and the recorded intent, so an owner's refusal closes the view, ends placement and answers the typed retryable code; the viewer's retry plans from Authority (§5's "one refusal, one success").
4. **The one answer that changes:** a remote owner whose plan differs at Authority now answers 409 with a `catalog_input_unavailable` body (before: the same 409 from the restore's binding check, bodiless), and the ingress maps that to `quality_catalog_unavailable` (before: `create_owner_transition`). Both are retryable 503s to the viewer.
5. **Confirm stays counted twice** (D3a decision 4, deferred to D3b). The counts are timed `TimedClient` operations; counting Confirm once means changing `execute_idempotent`/`source_layout_idempotent`'s timing in `hiqlite.rs`, a formally pinned file, and breaks the continuity of `plurx_http_store_reads_total{class="write"}` for no read saved. The acceptance spelling is the pinned 6/8/8/9/9 timed writes, which are 5/7/7/8/8 distinct durable writes; the pin's comment says so.
6. **A legacy route on a remote owner plans from Authority once** (`unbound`), rather than "as today" (each helper's own quorum read): the same freshness, one revision, fewer reads. The ingress confirms its own plan at Authority before handing such a start to a remote owner (review S2), so §4.4 holds on unbound routes too.
7. **No same-request plan for the burn guard.** `subtitles.stored_sources` (bound by D3t) is read by `burn_would_discard_this_session_hdr` and `burn_track_is_stored_empty` only on a burn request; it is not on §6.1's list and stays a Store read.
8. **The behavioural takeover-class guard (§8) is the source pin.** Takeover, resurrection, the prepared successor, the rate-control refresh and offline packaging cannot read the view (they live in `STAYS` files, pinned by the settings inventory) and every one of their calls into a planned helper passes `None` (pinned by call). A store double that answers the view differently would test a path none of them can reach; the mutation "hand takeover a plan" changes a pinned row of `PLAN_CALLS` and fails it.
9. **Key bound.** `PLANNING_SNAPSHOT_KEYS_MAX = 64` for the planning snapshot alone; `get_settings`' 32 and its store-contract refusal are unchanged.
10. **Co-author line.** This lane's commits carry `Claude Opus 5.5` (the model that built them).

11. **The owner's plan deadline** (review S4): `owner_plan` runs under `start_deadline.min(create_stage_deadline(2 s))`, the bound of the restore stage whose read it replaced; a timeout is a 503 the ingress's retry ladder already handles. It is the one timer D3b adds (contract §2 says none): an awaited request-path deadline, owning no task, producer or process, reviewed in `tests/playback/rolling-producer-owners.toml` (`namespaced-time-constructor` 2203 → 2204) on the coordinator's S4 ruling.
12. **Plan-derived scratch limits stay published process-wide** (review N2). `ahead_limits_for_start` publishes the start's scratch and ahead limits as the snapshot every session reads for the 2 s until the maintenance loop refreshes it from a quorum read. With a plan those limits are as old as the plan — the create's own start, under a second — and the binding covers the three keys; candidate routes have published a reused (ingress) snapshot this way since D4. The worst case is a save of an `hls_ahead_*`/`hls_scratch_*` key reaching other sessions one refresh later. Harmless; not scoped.
13. **The SQLite planning source** (review N1). On SQLite the store is the only copy; the Developer row says plans "read the store" there, and an owner's refused answer is counted once, as Authority.
14. **Census reach** (review N5). `deletes_rows_of` also counts `UPDATE OR REPLACE`. The writer census keeps its `plurx-core/store/` prefix exemption: a raw-client write anywhere under `store/` is refused by `every_settings_writer_is_floored_or_writes_no_replicated_row`'s raw-client scan (only bootstrap, install, provision and seal functions may write outside `TimedClient`), whatever table it writes, so the exemption covers only `TimedClient` writers, which arm both floors.
