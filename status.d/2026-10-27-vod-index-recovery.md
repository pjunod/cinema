# VOD index recovery

Owner Claude (Linux Dolby Vision session); [#1299](http://forge.lan:3000/noirr/plurx/pulls/1299). Large remuxes never got a VOD index, so every play fell back to live HLS: the index read's time budget ignored file size and a timed-out build was never retried yet reported "pending" forever (77 files on lab5), the local pass quit whenever a DV title scan held the background lane, and in the cluster a learner never resolved requests aimed at it while viewer requests were refused at the 4096-row queue cap. Fixed with a size- and rate-based budget, due/waiting/terminal outcomes with a distinct `vod_index_unavailable` reason, a queued local pass, and learner/viewer/removed-node queue handling; adversarial review's 12 findings fixed. Known limit: copy/VOD viewers without an admission do not preempt an index read. Deployed 2026-11-07 to media1, lab4 and lab6 (builds `4ddbf1229` and `edf6d08fa`, both contain it).
