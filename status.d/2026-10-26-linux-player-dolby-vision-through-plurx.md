# Linux player (Dolby Vision through plurx)

[plan and milestones](../docs/clients/LINUX-PLAYER-PLAN.md). Owner Claude (Linux Dolby Vision session). M1–M5 (#1285, #1288, #1290, #1291) merged to `main` by [#1295](http://forge.lan:3000/noirr/plurx/pulls/1295); not deployed. Package 0.3.0 verified in a fresh container and rebuilds byte-identical; on the ROG (CI runners paused) both the RTX and the Arc iGPU play 4K CM v4.0, CM v2.9 and FEL clips without dropped frames. Paul's first real playback (Avatar, from the web app) found four faults, fixed in 0.3.1 ([#1297](http://forge.lan:3000/noirr/plurx/pulls/1297)): no display from the portal, the NVIDIA driver's colour surface closing the window, control capabilities missing (400/409), and copies narrowing Profile 7 to HDR10 for a client that names it (server). Deployed 2026-10-27; Avatar and Twilight now play in Dolby Vision from the web app. 0.3.2 ([#1298](http://forge.lan:3000/noirr/plurx/pulls/1298)) adds the Profile 7 enhancement layer over HLS (mpv's lavf demuxer path never split it), the whole film on the seek bar of a live session (mpv patch 0005), and a package build that survives snapshot.ubuntu.com outages. Next: M6 acceptance on the ROG.
