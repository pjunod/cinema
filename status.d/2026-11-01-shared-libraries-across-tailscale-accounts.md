# Shared libraries across Tailscale accounts

[operating guide](../docs/features/SHARED-LIBRARIES-OPERATIONS.md#reading-connection-status). Owner Claude (claude-opus-5-5, session 01BCWL9y); [PR #1339](http://forge.lan:3000/noirr/plurx/pulls/1339). Root cause on the Mac: the LaunchAgent's Tailscale CLI calls were answered by the Tailscale GUI, so host setup was `unavailable` and outbound sharing dials had no source address; fixed with `TAILSCALE_BE_CLI=1` (verified on the Mac under launchd). Second cause: the fleet's Tailscale share was never accepted by the receiving account (root2you). Settings → Sharing now shows a connection checklist with the host account and failing step, per-connection reachability and diagnosis, Tailscale share acceptance, and a sharing activity log. Not deployed: Paul deploys plurxd to the fleet and the Mac app.
